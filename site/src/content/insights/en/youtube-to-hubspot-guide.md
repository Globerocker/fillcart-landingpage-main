**Major update:** HubSpot has released a new feature that makes it possible to **upload and manage YouTube videos directly from the HubSpot Marketing Hub**. A real upgrade, especially for teams working with Performance Max, video ads or organic YouTube marketing.

All details on the official rollout are in the [official HubSpot documentation](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/social/upload-and-publish-youtube-videos).

**Note:** The feature is only available to users with a **Marketing Hub Professional** or **Enterprise** subscription. Starter accounts and CRM Free do not have access.

## Why This Update Matters

Video is the medium of the moment, and YouTube is the second-largest search engine in the world. Whether webinars, demos or product clips: anyone who is not actively publishing this content today is giving away massive potential.

With the new update you can:

- Upload YouTube videos directly from HubSpot
- Manage copy, titles, thumbnails and playlists directly in HubSpot
- Easily integrate videos into campaigns
- Track performance data directly in the CRM

## Especially Attractive for Google Ads and PMax

For **Performance Max (PMax)** and **video ads on Google**, the video you use must be publicly hosted on YouTube. Until now that meant a real media break: record the video, export it, upload it to YouTube, embed it manually.

Now everything happens in one tool:

- Create the video and upload it to HubSpot
- Breeze (HubSpot Intelligence) generates suitable titles and descriptions
- Publish directly to YouTube with one click
- Immediately available for Google Ads

For product videos, case studies or short snippets on landing pages in particular, this is a huge boost in operational efficiency.

## How It Works

1. **Connect your YouTube channel:** Under Marketing, Social Media, Connect accounts, connect your official channel.
2. **Create a new post:** Select YouTube as the platform. HubSpot automatically switches to video mode.
3. **Choose a video from the library or upload a new one.**
4. **Add the details:** Title, description, category, language, custom thumbnail, playlist assignment, and set the "not made for kids" tag (mandatory).
5. **Schedule or publish immediately:** Including the option to link the post to a campaign.

## SEO and Organic Traffic

YouTube is also an underrated SEO channel. Videos rank more and more often in Google search results, especially for topics that need explanation. The integration lets you optimize videos in a targeted way and integrate them into your content strategy.

Recommended best practices:

- Structured titles with keywords
- Transcript in the description text
- Link to the relevant landing page
- Use a playlist structure for topic clusters

## A Real Use Case From My Team

A recent example: the product demo for a new feature at a European legal-tech software group was prepared with ChatGPT, edited in the video editor and published directly to YouTube via HubSpot. Including:

- Automatically generated description text
- Link to the booking page
- Integration into Google PMax ads

Result: within 24 hours, the first organic views and interactions were visible in the CRM.

## Conclusion: Video Is Gold, and Now the Workflow Is Good Too

With this YouTube integration, HubSpot takes a big step toward content distribution and performance marketing. The new connection saves time, reduces sources of error and helps you use existing material in a more targeted way.

Especially in combination with Google Ads, PMax, SEO and internal content workflows, you get a continuous funnel, from production to performance.

If you use Marketing Hub Pro or Enterprise, you should start using this feature regularly right away.

See the [official HubSpot guide](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/social/upload-and-publish-youtube-videos) for the step-by-step instructions.
