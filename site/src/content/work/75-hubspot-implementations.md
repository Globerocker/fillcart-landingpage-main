## Context

Before I ran marketing in house, I spent a year and a half as senior consultant at a HubSpot Diamond partner agency. The agency implemented HubSpot for B2B companies of all sizes. I owned implementations end to end, led a team of four consultants and ran my own key accounts, among them an energy utility and a testing and certification group.

## The problem

Every HubSpot implementation starts with the same question: what is a lead, who owns it and when does sales get it. Most clients had bought the software before answering it. The job was to turn a licence into a system the team actually uses, 75 times, each with a different sales process, a different data model and a different set of people who had to agree.

## What I did

- Ran discovery with founders and sales leads to define lifecycle, lead status, pipelines and the hand off between teams before building anything.
- Designed and built the portals: objects, properties, workflows, sequences, lead scoring, routing and reporting.
- Set up integrations with the clients' product and billing systems where the data had to flow both ways.
- Led four consultants: reviews, standards for documentation and the rule that the person who draws the architecture builds it.
- Trained client teams in their own portal, on their own deals, instead of with slides.

## What was built

75 HubSpot portals across Marketing, Sales, Service and CMS Hub, each documented with its data model, its lifecycle logic and the reasons behind every field. The key accounts ran on full implementations with routing, service level rules and dashboards for founders and sales. A set of internal standards for how the team scopes, builds and hands over a portal came out of that volume.

## Results

75 HubSpot implementations, counted as portals set up or rebuilt where I was the responsible consultant. Four consultants reported to me. 15 five star reviews are attributed to my implementations in the partner directory. The energy utility and the testing and certification group were my own accounts.
