## Context

Alongside my in house role I worked project based as senior CRM consultant for a HubSpot agency that serves SaaS and AI companies. These clients grow fast, change their sales process often and connect HubSpot to their own product and billing data. Each build was a custom specification, not a template.

## The problem

SaaS and AI companies rarely have a simple funnel. Product signups, trials, usage events and billing all carry signals that sales needs, and most portals ignore them. The standard HubSpot setup gives a form and a pipeline. The clients needed lead scoring on product usage, routing by segment, sequences triggered by behaviour and dashboards that founders trust. And they needed it in weeks, not quarters.

## What I did

- Ran the architecture sessions with founder and sales lead: objects, properties, lifecycle, pipelines with exit criteria.
- Specified and built lead scoring, routing, service level rules, sequences and workflows per client.
- Designed the integrations with product and billing stacks, through APIs and n8n, so usage and revenue data landed in HubSpot as properties.
- Worked inside an eight person delivery team on standards, reviews and documentation.
- Handed over each portal with documentation and a session for the client team.

## What was built

16 HubSpot portals, each with a documented data model, a lifecycle the client's sales team agreed to, scoring and routing on product data, and dashboards for founders and sales. Integrations run through APIs and n8n flows into HubSpot properties, not into attachments or exports. Each build took four to eight weeks from architecture session to hand over.

## Results

16 custom HubSpot builds were delivered for SaaS and AI companies, counted as portals built or rebuilt to a custom specification where I was the responsible consultant. The delivery team had eight people. Builds took four to eight weeks each, measured from the first architecture session to hand over.
